Default due intervals after initial learning: +1, +3, +7, +14, +30 calendar days. Store explicit dates. On a successful unaided recall, advance to the next planned stage; if overdue, review now and schedule the next interval from today. On a lapse or correct guess, explain briefly, retry later in-session, and schedule +1 day; this retry does not count as retained recall. Queue only a few high-value prompts per topic. Review due/overdue items before new material; reduce new material if the queue is unmanageable. These intervals are an adjustable study heuristic, not a medical claim.
