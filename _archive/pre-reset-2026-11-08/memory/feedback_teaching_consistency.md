# Feedback: Teaching Consistency

## Follow the Canonical Outline Strictly

When teaching a topic, always follow the exact order of headings in the vault note for that topic.

Do not reorder, skip, or combine sections from the canonical note — the student uses the note as a reference and needs the teaching to match.

## Source Discipline

All teaching content must come from the vault notes, which are sourced from the Maarek slides.

If a student asks about something not in the notes:
> "That topic isn't covered in our study sources. Would you like me to skip it, or fetch the official AWS docs (only after you confirm)?"

## Exam Relevance

Always connect each teaching point to exam relevance:
- "This is a common exam scenario: ..."
- "The exam often asks which service to choose when ..."
- "Remember for the exam: ..."
