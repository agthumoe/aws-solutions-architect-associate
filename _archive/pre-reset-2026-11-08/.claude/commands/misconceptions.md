# Top Misconceptions & Gotchas

Surface the most common mistakes and traps for a topic.

**Usage:** `/misconceptions <topic>` or `/gotchas <topic>`  
**Examples:** `/misconceptions rag`, `/gotchas bedrock`, `/misconceptions domain4`

## Instructions

Given `$ARGUMENTS` as the topic:

1. Read the relevant vault notes.

2. Identify and list the **top 5–8 misconceptions** students have about this topic. For each:

   ```
   ❌ WRONG: "[The misconception]"
   ✅ CORRECT: "[The truth]"
   💡 WHY IT MATTERS: "[Exam consequence if you believe the wrong version]"
   ```

3. After the misconceptions list, provide **3 self-test questions** that specifically target these traps:
   - Multiple choice questions where the wrong answer is tempting
   - The correct answer must require knowing the distinction

4. End with a **memory hook** for the hardest distinction (one sentence the student can repeat to themselves).

## Style
- Be blunt about the traps. "This one gets people. Here's why."
- Every misconception must be sourced from vault notes — no invented traps.
