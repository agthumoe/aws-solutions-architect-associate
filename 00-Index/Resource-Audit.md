## Evidence and limits

- Public route observations: `docs/resource-http-checks.json` (preserves actual transport failures and alternate access).
- GitHub repository metadata: `docs/resource-repositories.json` (archive flag, last push, license where detected); metadata does not establish correctness or grant rights to unrelated course material.
- Publisher confirmation for course/video: [freeCodeCamp article](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/pass-the-aws-certified-solutions-architect-associate-certification/).
- [Official AWS practice descriptions](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certification-prep/) distinguish free sets from subscribed full exams.
- [Official AWS exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-associate-03/solutions-architect-associate-03.html) overrides third-party version/weight claims.

This is a resource-level and targeted content audit, not a transcript-by-transcript or question-by-question review of every external collection. Selected local factual corrections are recorded in [[docs/content-audit]]. If an excluded site later fixes its errors, re-evaluate from evidence before restoring it. Do not silently add unchecked third-party facts to the vault.
